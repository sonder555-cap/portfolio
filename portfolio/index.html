CAPSTONE · WEB SYSTEM 01

CoraVergel Resort

A complete booking and management platform for a resort in Tigbauan, Iloilo. Guests browse rooms, check live availability and reserve from the public site, while staff manage the same inventory from an admin dashboard. Bookings that overlap an existing stay are rejected on the server, so the same room can't be sold twice, and accounts are verified through one-time codes sent over email.
